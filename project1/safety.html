Minimum Cooking Temperatures for Meats

Below are the minimum cooking temperatures for many types of meat. These are generalized, and the exact safe minimum temperature may vary based on the size of your ingredients.

MeatMinimal Temperature
Beef, Pork, Veal and Lamb145 degrees F (62.8 degrees C)
Ham, Fresh or Smoked (Uncooked)145 degrees F (62.8 degrees C)
All Poultry (Breasts, Whole Bird, Legs, Thighs, Wings, Ground Poultry, Giblets and Stuffing)165 degrees F (73.9 degrees C)
Ground Meats160 degrees F (71.1 degrees C)
Eggs160 degrees F (71.1 degrees C)
Fish and Shellfish145 degrees F (62.8 degrees C)
Casseroles165 degrees F (73.9 degrees C)

Note: For beef, pork, veal, lamb, and ham, allow the meat to rest after cooking for at least three minutes.
