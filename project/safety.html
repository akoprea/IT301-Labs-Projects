Quick Tips

  • Keep a fire extinguisher and a first aid kit nearby to stop fires and treat small injuries.
  • To prevent contamination between raw meats and other foods: always wash cutting boards, knives and utensils, and countertops with hot, soapy water. I prefer to handle all meats last when preparing for a recipe to minimize this risk.
  • Don't leave perishable foot out for more than two hours at room temperature.
  • Extinguish grease fires (fires in a pot or pan) by covering with a lid and removing from heat. Do not use water.

Minimum Cooking Temperatures for Meats

Below are the minimum cooking temperatures for many types of meat. These are generalized, and the exact safe minimum temperature may vary based on the size of your ingredients.

MeatMinimal Temperature
Beef, Pork, Veal, Lamb, and Ham145 degrees F (62.8 degrees C)
Fish and Shellfish145 degrees F (62.8 degrees C)
Ground Meats, and Eggs160 degrees F (71.1 degrees C)
All Poultry (Breasts, Whole Bird, Legs, Thighs, Wings, Ground Poultry, Giblets and Stuffing)165 degrees F (73.9 degrees C)

Note: For beef, pork, veal, lamb, and ham, allow the meat to rest after cooking for at least three minutes.
