Cuban Sliders

I like to make these when I'm feeling lazy or am in a rush.

Ingredients

  • 2 pkg. (12 oz each) Hawaiian sweet rolls
  • 1 1/4 lbs thinly sliced deli ham
  • 10 slices slices swiss or gouda cheese
  • 24 dill pickle slices (about 1 jar)
  • finely sliced onion to taste
  • dijon mustard to taste
  • light mayo to taste
  • butter to taste

Cooking Directions

  1. Cut all of the Hawaiian rolls in half. Place the bottoms in a well greased casserole dish.
  2. Layer the mayo, ham, cheese, pickles, onion, and mustard into the dish.
  3. Add the tops of the buns. Melt the butter and brush it over the buns. Cover with foil.
  4. Bake at 350 degrees F for 10 minutes. Remove the foil. Cook for 10 more minutes.
